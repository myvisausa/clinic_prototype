# Issues found

Ordered by how visible the failure would be in front of the clinic. This list only records observed behavior.

1. **Consent template selects an unexpected grantor.** Path: `Documentos → Nuevo documento → Categoría Consentimientos → Plantilla Consentimiento informado odontologico general`. For the fake patient, observed a different person as **Otorgante**. The flow was stopped and cancelled before creating a document; no agreement was signed. Screenshot: pending masked capture.
2. **WhatsApp reconnection cannot prepare a QR.** Path: `Reconectar → /es/setup/whatsapp`. Expected a QR preparation path; observed: `No pudimos preparar WhatsApp. Inténtalo de nuevo.` No QR was generated and no phone was paired. Screenshot: `images/07-whatsapp-error-04.png`.
3. **No professional is available while booking.** Path: `Agenda diaria → Nueva cita → Profesional`. Expected a selectable professional; observed an empty list. The appointment was cancelled and not saved, so Calendar and day-of-visit states could not be tested. Screenshot: `images/02-profesional-vacio-03.png`.
4. **Team, schedules, and blocking lack selectable people/resources.** `Ajustes → Equipo` showed `No se encontraron datos`; `Horarios y disponibilidad → Horario` showed no resources; `Calendario → Bloquear horario` asked for an Anfitrión but offered no choices. No edits or block were saved. Screenshots: `images/07-equipo-vacio-01.png`, `images/08-horarios-vacio-01.png`, `images/08-bloquear-sin-anfitrion-02.png`.
5. **Saved odontogram planned-item professional is an identifier instead of a name.** Path: `Odontograma → Evolución de procedimientos`. Expected a readable professional name; observed a UUID in the DOM; the comparison-viewport screenshot crops that cell. Screenshot for context: `images/04-odontograma-guardado-09.png`.
6. **Required parallel-run fields remain unverified.** Documento was visible on the patient form, but no Tipo de documento was observed. The clinical note has Entidad and free-text Impresión diagnóstica; coded diagnosis entry was not verified. Screenshot: `images/06-documento-demografia-02.png`, `images/04-historia-05.png`.
7. **The captured viewport clips parts of action areas.** At 1686×948, the patient editor and the odontogram evolution area are not fully visible. No crop or scale was applied; live verification is needed before treating this as an app-layout defect. Screenshot context: `images/06-documento-demografia-02.png`, `images/04-odontograma-guardado-09.png`.
